# elektrojob — Design Context

## Users
Swiss-German tradespeople (Elektriker, Elektroinstallateure, Lehrlinge) and recent vocational graduates aged ~18–55. They search for jobs primarily on phones, often during commute, breaks, or evenings on the couch. Limited patience for long forms. They do NOT think of themselves as "users of an app" — they think of themselves as workers looking for the next job. The site has to feel like a job-finding utility, not a tech product.

## Brand Personality
**Three words:** practical, direct, dependable.

The voice is plain Swiss-German-flavoured High German: short sentences, no jargon, no English where a German word exists. Feels like a fair employer talking to a candidate, not a marketer. Confidence without pomp. The site already uses warm amber `#fba918` (oklch 0.795 0.155 75) — a colour from the world of high-vis vests and service-van branding, not a tech brand.

## Aesthetic Direction
**Match the existing site exactly.** Light theme, amber primary, Plus Jakarta Sans, soft rounded corners (`--radius: 0.625rem` rising to `2xl`/`3xl` for prominent surfaces), composited transitions on cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1), generous whitespace, Tailwind 4 + shadcn/ui primitives.

The Swipe feature is a NEW MODE inside the same brand — not a different brand. Cards are larger and more confident than search results, but they live in the same colour, font, radius, and shadow system. The differentiator is **motion**: the rest of the site is static and form-based; the swipe surface uses momentum, physics, and haptics. That alone makes it memorable without redesigning the brand.

## Design Principles
1. **One thumb, one decision.** Every screen must be operable with one hand. Primary actions sit in the lower 60% of the viewport. No fixed top action bars below the fold.
2. **Show, don't ask.** Never request information already inferable (location → from PLZ; salary range → from job; name → optional, derive from email if needed).
3. **Motion lives where decisions live.** The Swipe deck moves with momentum, spring, and rotation; everywhere else stays calm. Don't sprinkle bounce on landing pages — earn it on the deck.
4. **Haptics confirm intent, never narrate.** Selection on touch-down, success on apply, error on validation failure. No haptics on entrances or scroll.
5. **Empty states sell the next action.** "Keine Jobs mehr in 25 km" must immediately offer the radius extension, not a sad illustration.
6. **Respect reduced motion.** Every animation falls back to instant when `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`. The swipe gesture is replaced with two large buttons.
